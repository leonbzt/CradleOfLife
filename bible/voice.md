# The Voice

Wry, knowing, competent, accurate (VISION.md §2a). The narrator is a sharp
strategy-game player narrating four billion years of *real* evolution as a grind.
The humour comes from the **frame** — treating brutal, factual biology as balance
decisions — never from zany invention.

Two dialects, one voice:

- **TierZoo:** "S-tier," "underrated pick," "this build got nerfed," "the meta."
- **r/outside & the frame:** "the Devs," "RNG," "grinding," "patch notes."

The balancing authority is always **the Devs** (natural selection). They are
impersonal, balance-obsessed, dry, and not on your side. They never explain and
never apologise.

---

## Do

- Present accurate biology *as game balance*. The joke is funny because it's true.
- Let the Dev/patch frame bind the registers together.
- Stay dry. Deadpan beats a punchline.
- Earnestness breaks through **once per age**, at the summit, delivered flat:
  *"No build has ever stood here. Good luck."*

## Don't

- Don't invent capabilities. Stylistic licence governs *naming and presentation*,
  never mechanics (VISION.md §2). If it isn't real, it doesn't ship.
- Don't do slapstick or memes-for-memes'-sake. Wit, not zany.
- Don't use loss-aversion framing ("you're losing progress"). Banned (VISION.md
  §12). The Devs dispatch; they don't nag.
- Don't break the fourth wall *out* of the frame. The frame *is* the fourth wall.

**Every line still passes gate 2.** Voice never licenses a false claim.

> Aerobic respiration now scales far better than the old anaerobic builds; if you
> were running sulfur metabolism, your numbers just fell off. The Devs have not
> commented. *(Grounded: rising marine oxygen and aerobic metabolism. Verify.)*

> Defense values across the shallows are rebalanced upward; pure glass-cannon
> grazers are feeling it. Yes, this is an arms race. No, it will not be reverted.
> *(Grounded: the Cambrian biomineralization arms race. Verify.)*

> **Hotfix — A cold snap.** Shallow productivity is down this patch. Filter
> feeders are eating less; the apex tier is unbothered, as usual. *(Grounded:
> glaciation-driven productivity drops. Verify.)*

## Example creature blurbs

> **Anomalocaris** — S-tier apex of the launch meta. Compound eyes that actually
> resolve the murk, paired grasping appendages, and the reach to use them. Holds
> the pinnacle drop. You are not beating this on day one.

> **Trilobite Grazer** — the durable mid-tier pick. Mineralized armour, rolls into
> a ball, waits you out. Boring? It outlasted nearly everything. Respect the
> defensive build.

> **Sea Anemone** — looks AFK. Isn't. The nematocyst proc fires the instant
> you're in range, and it stacks. Underrated DoT in a meta obsessed with burst.

*Every blurb above states only real, well-established traits. The biology is the
joke; keep it true.*
