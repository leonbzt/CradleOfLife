# Evolutionary Idle RPG / TierZoo-Inspired Design Notes

## Core RPG Systems

### Adaptive Radiation

A lineage branches into many specialized subclasses after entering new environments.

* Evolution trees
* Biome specialization
* Prestige branching
* Divergent playstyles

---

### Coevolution / Arms Race

Predators, prey, parasites, and defenses constantly escalate against each other.

* Rotating metas
* Counter-builds
* Trait escalation
* Seasonal balance shifts

---

### Mass Extinction Events

Periodic world resets reshape ecosystems and progression.

* Prestige wipes
* Global biome collapse
* Rare mutation opportunities
* New evolutionary eras

---

### Succession

Ecosystems regrow and evolve over time after catastrophe.

* Dynamic maps
* Terrain progression
* Resource regeneration
* Civilization/ecosystem rebuilding loops

---

## Core Classes / Archetypes

### Apex Predator

High power, high upkeep.

Traits:

* Burst DPS
* Territory dominance
* Fear/intimidation mechanics

Examples:

* Tiger
* Orca
* Tyrannosaurus

---

### Mesopredator

Flexible survivalist generalists.

Traits:

* Adaptability
* Scavenging
* Efficient resource use

Examples:

* Foxes
* Coyotes
* Monitor lizards

---

### Ecosystem Engineer

Species that reshape environments.

Traits:

* Passive biome buffs
* Infrastructure creation
* Automation scaling

Examples:

* Beavers
* Corals
* Earthworms

---

### Eusocial Colony

Swarm-based superorganisms.

Traits:

* Worker specialization
* Exponential scaling
* Collective intelligence
* Unit spawning economies

Examples:

* Ants
* Bees
* Termites

---

## Evolutionary Traits as RPG Mechanics

### Mimicry

Pretending to be another species.

Gameplay:

* Stealth
* Threat spoofing
* Deception builds
* Infiltration mechanics

Examples:

* Hoverflies
* Orchid mantises

---

### Aposematism

Warning coloration and intimidation.

Gameplay:

* Retaliation damage
* Fear aura
* Toxic deterrence

Examples:

* Poison dart frogs
* Wasps

---

### Symbiosis

Mutually beneficial species partnerships.

Gameplay:

* Companion systems
* Shared buffs
* Resource linking
* Fusion evolution

Examples:

* Cleaner fish + sharks
* Mycorrhizal fungi + plants

---

### Living Fossils

Ancient stable lineages resistant to change.

Gameplay:

* Meta resistance
* Ancient traits
* Survival bonuses
* Stability builds

Examples:

* Coelacanths
* Horseshoe crabs

---

### Keystone Species

Species with outsized ecosystem influence.

Gameplay:

* Global passive modifiers
* Ecosystem balance powers
* Territory influence systems

Examples:

* Wolves
* Sea otters

---

## Ecology Systems

### r/K Selection Theory

#### r-Strategists

Fast, fragile, high reproduction.

Gameplay:

* Swarm builds
* Rapid expansion
* Disposable units

Examples:

* Insects
* Rodents

#### K-Strategists

Slow, durable, elite species.

Gameplay:

* Expensive units
* Strong scaling
* Long lifespan bonuses

Examples:

* Elephants
* Whales

---

### Trophic Levels

Progression through the food chain.

Example ladder:

* Microbe
* Herbivore
* Small predator
* Pack hunter
* Apex predator

Gameplay:

* Evolution ascension
* Resource hierarchy
* Predator-prey loops

---

### Metamorphosis

Distinct life stages with different gameplay roles.

Gameplay:

* Temporary classes
* Phase transitions
* Rebirth systems
* Multi-stage progression

Examples:

* Caterpillar → butterfly
* Tadpole → frog

## Overall Gameplay Structure

1. Ecosystems emerge through succession
2. Species diversify through adaptive radiation
3. Niches create specialized builds
4. Coevolution drives the meta
5. Keystone species reshape the world
6. Symbiosis unlocks fusion/synergy systems
7. Mass extinctions reset progression into new eras
